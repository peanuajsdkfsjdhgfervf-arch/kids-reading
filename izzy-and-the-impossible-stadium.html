Izzy and the Impossible Stadium

1. One More Page

Izzy was still in bed when he found the impossible stadium.

Not outside his window. Inside the story on his Kobo.

It had a hockey rink, a soccer field and a basketball court. Its roof was stuck shut.

Izzy grinned. He knew exactly who could fix that.

“Raph! Ruth! I have an idea!”

Raph appeared in the doorway. “Does it involve cleaning up?”

“It involves Lego.”

“Excellent,” said Raph. “The opposite.”

2. Mission Control

By breakfast, their own Lego stadium covered the rug.

Ruth built a roof that folded open. Raph built the goals. Izzy added a tiny knight with a whistle.

“The referee,” he explained.

“Why does he have a sword?” asked Ruth.

“Very strict rules.”

They invented a new adventure: six sports tokens would unlock the roof. Each token was a different Lego brick. Everything that happened next belonged to their made-up story.

Izzy tapped the knight. “First stop: the Storm!”

In their imaginations, the little stadium grew enormous.

3. Storm Warning

Izzy stood in the hockey crease wearing his Storm goalie gear.

A player raced toward him. The puck vanished behind a stick, then came flying low.

Izzy dropped into a butterfly. His pads covered the bottom of the net.

THUMP!

The puck bounced safely away. Raph cheered from behind the boards. Ruth held up one finger.

“First token!”

Izzy raised his catcher. The tiny knight referee, now perched on the scoreboard, nearly fell off celebrating.

“Someone get that guy a helmet,” said Raph.

4. Different Gear, Same Storm

The Storm had two goalies. Now the other goalie took the net, and Izzy switched to playing out.

“Your turn to stop them,” Izzy said, tapping his teammate's glove.

He joined the next shift, skating with the puck toward the corner.

Two defenders followed. Izzy spotted a teammate beside the net and slid a pass between their sticks.

Tap. Shot. Goal!

Ruth handed him the second token.

“I thought you were going to shoot,” said Raph.

“So did they,” said Izzy.

5. Flying Hornet

The ice became grass. Izzy was wearing his Hornets soccer shirt.

Raph sent a high pass toward him. Izzy watched it drop, leaned back and launched the bicycle kick he loved trying as a striker.

One foot swung up. The other struck the ball.

THWACK!

The ball flew into the goal. Izzy landed on the grass and looked up.

Ruth was applauding. The knight was lying on his back too.

“He's copying you,” she said.

“Third token,” said Izzy. “And a terrible referee.”

6. The Flying Save

“My turn to shoot,” said Raph.

Izzy pulled on goalkeeper gloves. He loved this job just as much as being a striker.

Raph aimed toward the far corner. Izzy pushed off, stretched sideways and reached with both gloves.

For one wonderful moment, he seemed to hang in the air.

His hands met the ball and pushed it past the post.

“You weren't supposed to reach that!” Raph laughed.

“I didn't ask the ball,” said Izzy.

Ruth produced token four. The knight's whistle had somehow become a tiny flower.

7. The Long Way Home

The stadium doors opened onto their neighbourhood. Three bicycles waited outside.

“Long way home?” asked Ruth. Everyone fastened their helmets.

They rode past familiar gardens and waved to a neighbour. At the park, token five waited beside the basketball court.

One socket left.

Izzy bounced the basketball. Raph guarded him. Ruth waited near the hoop.

Pass to Ruth. Pass back. Izzy shot.

The ball circled the rim so slowly that all three held their breath.

Then it dropped through. “SIX!”

8. The Roof Opens

Back on the rug, Izzy pressed six Lego bricks into six waiting spaces.

Ruth lifted the stadium roof. Inside, Raph had hidden a little bedroom.

A tiny Izzy lay in a Lego bed, holding a flat grey brick.

“His Kobo,” said Raph. “Obviously.”

That night, the real Izzy tucked himself into bed and opened his own Kobo. Six adventures were plenty for one imaginary day.

He read until he felt sleepy, then left the Kobo beside his pillow.

Tomorrow morning, before getting up, there would be another page.

And perhaps another impossible stadium.
