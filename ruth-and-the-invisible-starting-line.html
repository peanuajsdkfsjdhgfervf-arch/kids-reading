Ruth and the Invisible Starting Line

The Bus-Stop Championships

Ruth was thirteen, and she could turn almost anything into a track-and-field event.

A long hallway? Sprint practice.

A patch of grass? A place to practise her long jump.

Waiting for the city bus with Raph? Apparently, that was training too.

“Stand on one leg,” she said.

Raph looked up from his notebook. “Why?”

“Balance.”

“I'm preparing for a World Cup debate.”

“You can debate on one leg.”

He tried. His backpack tipped him sideways.

Ruth caught his sleeve.

“Excellent start,” she said. “Very dramatic.”

They went to the same school and took the city bus together. Ruth checked the bus number before they climbed aboard. Raph checked whether she was ready to hear why his favourite soccer team was better than Izzy's.

She was not.

He told her anyway.

Not Just Fast

At lunch, they headed to the school track.

Raph stood beside Ruth's lane with a stopwatch.

“Ready?” he asked.

Ruth settled her feet behind the starting line.

She loved this bit: the quiet second before everything moved.

“Go!”

She ran.

Past the first bend. Past the empty stands. All the way to the finish.

Raph pressed the button.

Then he looked worried.

“I think I stopped it when you were nearly finished.”

“How nearly?”

“A helpful amount.”

Ruth laughed and bent over, catching her breath.

Track and field wasn't only about being fast. It was about trying again, even when your legs felt like wet noodles—or your timer was Raph.

“One more,” she said. “This time, wait until I cross the line.”

Raph held up the watch. “You've got this!”

Everybody Gets a Lane

That afternoon, Izzy joined them in the park with a hockey stick.

“Can my event involve this?” he asked.

Raph brought a soccer ball.

Ruth considered her unusual team.

“First, a relay.”

They used a rolled-up sock as a baton.

Raph ran his stretch. Izzy took the sock and sprinted. Ruth raced the final stretch, then turned to cheer them through another round.

Nobody remembered to time it.

Nobody particularly minded.

Ruth held the sock above her head.

“New team record!”

“What record?” asked Izzy.

“Most fun while carrying laundry.”
